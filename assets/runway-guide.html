What is Runway?

Runway is your private financial tracker inside The ARC portal. It tells you exactly how long your money lasts at your current pace — your runway before you either take off or run out of road.

Log your dues, your daily spending, and any money coming in. Runway does the rest — calculating your burn rate, days left, net flow, and debt-free date automatically.

Setup — 4 Steps

1. Set Your Credit Limit

Enter the total money available to you this period — salary, savings, freelance income, all of it. This is your baseline. Everything is calculated against this number.

2. Debt Ledger — Fixed Obligations

Log everything you must pay: rent, EMIs, subscriptions, loan repayments. Enter the amount Due and how much you've already Paid. The balance is auto-calculated.

ColumnWhat to enter
NameWhat the payment is for — e.g. ROOM RENT, AXIS EMI
DueTotal amount owed
PaidAmount already paid
BalanceAuto-calculated. Goes green when cleared.

3. Daily Bleed Log — Variable Spend

Every time you spend outside of your fixed dues, log it here. Pick a date, add a description, choose a category, enter the amount.

CategoryWhat it covers
FEEOne-time charges, platform or onboarding fees
SUBRecurring subscriptions — Netflix, Spotify, SaaS tools
FOODAll food and drink
TRAVELCabs, fuel, flights, transit
FUNEntertainment, events, anything recreational
SHOPPurchases, gear, clothing, online orders
OTHERAnything else

REPEAT flag: if the same description appears more than once, Runway marks it — a sign of a recurring charge you may have forgotten.

4. Received Funds — Money In

Log any incoming money — freelance payments, refunds, transfers, side income. This is added to your available credit automatically.

Financial Metrics — Explained

🔥 Daily Burn Rate
Total spend ÷ days logged
How much you spend per day on average. If your burn is ₹800/day and you have ₹12,000 left — that's 15 days, not a month.
🛬 Runway Days Left
Available credit ÷ daily burn rate
How many days your money will last at current pace. Under 30 days triggers a red alert.
📊 Net Cash Flow
Received − Total Spend
Green = more came in than went out. Red = you're burning faster than you're earning. The most honest number on the dashboard.
💳 Credit Utilisation
(Dues + Spend) ÷ Credit × 100
How much of your total credit you've consumed. Above 80% is a danger zone — almost no buffer left.
💰 Savings Ratio
Remaining credit ÷ Credit Limit × 100
What percentage of your income you're keeping after all expenses. Negative means you're living beyond your means.
📅 Freedom Countdown
Enter monthly payoff amount
Enter how much you can put toward dues each month. Runway calculates your debt-free date — a real date, not an abstract goal.

Your data is private.
Everything you log in Runway is stored privately against your Google account. It is not visible to other members, the ARC team, or anyone else. No one can access your financial data — not even the owner of this platform.
