About the Author
Revanth Mapakshi
Design Engineer → Entrepreneur · Mentor · Builder

I grew up watching my father run a business. Some years he had money, some years he didn't — but he always had one thing: freedom of time and choice. That stayed with me.

I became a design engineer. I worked in Bangalore, then Hyderabad. Good salary, good trajectory, good on paper. But I did the math one day and realised: saving through a corporate salary alone would take a decade to build what I wanted. That realization made me go a little mad — in the best way.

I took a loan. I started. I built The ARC to give other professionals the system I wish I had found before I needed to figure it out the hard way.
